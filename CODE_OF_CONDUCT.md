# Code of Conduct

TrustDex welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while still asking for evidence.
- Respect privacy and never pressure someone to disclose credentials, private configurations, or security-report details.
- Help keep technical discussions focused and accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

For a confidential conduct report, contact @grigent through a private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue asking for contact without including sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
